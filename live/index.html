Your cursor becomes the model's gaze

Qwen3-VL-2B runs entirely in this tab on WebGPU. Move the cursor over the scan and about a hundred of its attention heads are pointed at the spot under it while it writes; move mid-sentence and the sentence follows. Scroll to grow or shrink the spotlight. The model is never told a region in words — the prompt is identical wherever you point.

What is and is not happening

Nothing is precomputed except the image's vision features. The language model decodes live on your GPU. Steering is one additive bias on the attention scores of the selected heads: +δ on image tokens inside the spotlight, −δ on the rest. No weights change.

Strength. We measured localisation saturating around 8 and text collapsing into repetition above 16; the value inherited from prior work is 10,000. The slider stops at 16.

The browser model is slightly weaker than the real one. Qwen3-VL also injects vision features at three early layers (“DeepStack”); the ONNX build has no inputs for them, so they are dropped here. Captions on the cluster are a little better.

Scans: SLAKE (Liu et al., 2021), open access. Model weights: an Apache-2.0 ONNX export of Qwen3-VL-2B with steering inputs, published by Gandikota & Bau, whose work on gaze heads in comic strips this study extends to medical imaging. Chest X-ray corpora under a PhysioNet DUA are absent by design.
